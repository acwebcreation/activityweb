Who we are

An active partner in Africa's economic growth

ILMALIA is convinced that Africa has all the potential to become the center of tomorrow's world development — and works to bring that potential to life.

  • Philosophy Concentrating expertise to give private and public sectors the means to reach that goal, growing the middle class as a lever for economic growth.
  • Mission Bring together stakeholders from around the world who believe in Africa's evolution, to carry out development projects across many fields.
  • Standards All funds handled are clear, clean and of non-criminal origin, free of fraud, laundering or terrorism financing links.
